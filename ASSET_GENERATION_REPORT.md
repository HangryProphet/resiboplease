## Generated Assets

Thirteen approved app assets were generated and integrated:

- seven reusable city-issue scenes under `assets/images/issues/`;
- three environmental CityBook banners under `assets/images/citybook/`; and
- three CityBook/Campaign Trail subject images under
  `assets/images/citybook/`.

The image-generation masters were PNG. Because these scenes are fully opaque
and a WebP encoder was unavailable, the shipped copies use quality-88 JPEG,
which is allowed by the asset brief. Packaging reduced the new app payload from
about 45 MB of PNG copies to 7.1 MB while retaining the approved visible style.

Two first-pass civilian scenes were rejected because reference-sheet influence
made background people resemble canonical candidates. Only the regenerated
civilian-only safety and cost-of-living scenes were copied into the project.

### Generation mode and prompt sets

- **Mode:** built-in ImageGen, one call per distinct image, using the approved
  style sheet and/or existing Bayhaven art as references.
- **Issue set:** systemic procurement/public funds, unfinished roadworks,
  delayed government services, education capacity, community safety, garbage
  collection, and household cost of living.
- **Banner set:** text-free environmental branding for Maya's clinic theme,
  Julian's jobs/port theme, and Victor's water/engineering theme.
- **Post set:** civilian mobile-clinic operations, a port-side jobs and skills
  event, and a community water-repair planning session.
- **Shared constraints:** fictional Bayhaven; hand-inked editorial-cartoon
  style; bold outlines; cel shading; warm print palette; mobile-readable central
  subject; no readable text, real politicians, logos, party marks, UI, borders,
  or watermarks. Candidate-specific faces were deliberately excluded from new
  scenes so existing canonical portraits remain the only identity masters.

## TV Broadcast Addendum

One additional asset was generated for the implemented TV Broadcast feature:

- `assets/images/tv/reporter_r8.png` — an original fictional civic-news robot
  reporter with a transparent background and a dark mouth panel designed for a
  lightweight code-driven talking indicator.

The asset was produced with built-in ImageGen using the approved Resibo style
sheet as a visual reference. The prompt specified a calm, trustworthy retro
civic-news robot; hand-inked editorial-cartoon lines; navy, teal, cream, muted
gold, and restrained red; no readable text, logos, real people, candidates,
watermarks, or recognizable copyrighted robots. A flat chroma background was
removed with the ImageGen skill's transparency helper before the final RGBA PNG
was integrated. Reporter bobbing and mouth light are Flutter animations, so no
extra animation frames or large video files are shipped.
